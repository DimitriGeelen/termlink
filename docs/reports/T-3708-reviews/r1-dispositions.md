| 1 | BLOCKER: `now_ms` swapped for 56 call sites; stamps sent or stored stop being wall time; suspend; restart; switch does not undo it | **Accepted, fixed** | `store::now_ms` is wall time again. Deadlines are ages: `fields::age_ms(stamp, now)` subtracts every wall jump the process saw after it first aged the stamp (jumps = wall vs monotonic disagreement > 2 s between looks; looks every heartbeat tick and every age). Stamps are classified by the jump count when first seen, because wall values cannot say which side of a backward jump a stamp is on. Used by `deliver.rs` (hand-over, answer) and the sender labels in `status_at`. Residual: a jump inside the tick in which a stamp is first seen, or while the process is down, is not corrected (skew shows the latter). |
