## Findings (what is needed)

F-1 (card 7.3 and 4.f, approval-broker scope) — AL-43 consume races with cancel, revoke and expiry. In 06b 2.3.c,
steps 3 to 6 (state == APPROVED check -> consumed_set insert -> state = CONSUMED) are not done under `event.lock` and are
not atomic with the event state. AL-42 takes event.lock only in `decide`, and its expiry sweeper and cancel/revoke can
move APPROVED to a final state between step 3 and step 6. Step 6 then overwrites a final state with CONSUMED and
executes. That breaks AL-42 invariant (2), "final states never change", and D-1.12, which draws the insert itself as the
transition. The card names exactly this case in 4.f: "revocation during a check, time-of-check to time-of-use". AL-43
2.3.f covers replay, two executors, backup restore and crash, but not this. Needed: make the APPROVED->CONSUMED
transition a single conditional step at the hub (for example, compare-and-set under event.lock together with the insert),
add the edge case, and add an FCW for "state no longer APPROVED at insert".

F-2 (card 7.3 and 4.f, approval-broker scope "check a grant") — AL-46 has no concurrency case. Two concurrent starts
can both read `u.starts < budget` (step 3) and both append (step 4), which exceeds the budget. A grant revoked or
expired between steps 2 and 5 is a TOCTOU the card names, and it is not addressed. Needed: a concurrency item that
makes the check and the log append one atomic step (or a counter insert with a limit), plus the revocation-during-check
case.

F-3 (card 4.g) — AL-44's recover() contradicts its crash table. The table (06b 2.4.f) says that for a crash between
the insert and the dispatch row, or between the dispatch row and the apply, "recovery checks `already_done`, applies if
not". But recover() iterates only "consumed approval with dispatch and no outcome", and for those it goes straight to
the post-condition, which reports FAILED for an effect that was never applied. It does not handle a consumed approval
with no dispatch row at all. Needed: write recover() so it matches the table (including the no-dispatch-row case), or
correct the table.

F-4 (card 7.3) — concurrency is missing for other state-changing algorithms. 06 10.3 itself lists concurrency only
for AL-1, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14, 15, 17, 21, 22, 23, 26, 27, 30, 31, 33, 35, 40, 42, 43 and 44, and says "the rest
with a one-line crash statement". 7.3 asks for concurrency and crash cases for every state-changing step. Algorithms
that change state and have no concurrency item include AL-36 (gap wait: held_since, release), AL-37 (owed list and
ladder), AL-45 (policy install racing a grant check), AL-49 (two membership events racing for the same epoch + 1),
AL-50 (break-glass), AL-51 (custody release, "at most one release per approval") and AL-52. Needed: a concurrency item
for each, or an explicit "single-writer, serialised by X" statement.

F-5 (card 5.D.1 and 7.4, minor) — D-1 asks for "one per stateful algorithm". AL-35 (UNRECONCILED, clean pass,
"unreconciled" label) is neither drawn nor listed in 06 7.0.1 as stateless. AL-36 has a held, released and
released-marked lifecycle but is listed as stateless. Needed: draw them, or justify why they are not stateful.

F-6 (card 5.1 test vectors, minor) — 06 5.6.c.1 gives the SI-1 line as "(152 bytes, then Enter)". The line as written,
and as composed by compose(), is 150 bytes (python len of the UTF-8 bytes = 150). Needed: correct the count, because a
test that asserts 152 fails against a correct build.
